Why Ganesh

About Lord Ganesh

Lord Ganesh — recognized by his elephant head — is one of the most loved deities in Hinduism, revered as the remover of obstacles and the god of wisdom, success, and new beginnings.

Son of Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati, he is invoked at the start of ceremonies and new ventures for blessings and good fortune.

During Ganesh Chaturthi, families install idols at home and in public pandals, offering prayers, sweets, and music for ten days before immersion.

Eco-friendly idols made of clay or natural materials protect the rivers and soil that receive them — a small choice with a lasting effect.
